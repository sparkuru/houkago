## Raw UI/UX Pro Max design-system output

Query: `social watch room queue management accessible responsive`

### Pattern

- **Name:** Hero + Testimonials + CTA
- **Conversion Focus:** Social proof before CTA. Use 3-5 testimonials. Include photo + name + role. CTA after social proof.
- **CTA Placement:** Hero (sticky) + Post-testimonials
- **Color Strategy:** Hero: Brand color. Testimonials: Light bg `#F5F5F5`. Quotes: italic, muted color `#666`. CTA: vibrant.
- **Sections:** Hero, problem statement, solution overview, testimonials carousel, CTA.

### Style

- **Name:** Vibrant & Block-based
- **Mode Support:** light and dark
- **Keywords:** bold, energetic, playful, block layout, geometric shapes, high color contrast, duotone, modern
- **Accessibility note:** ensure WCAG compliance

### Typography and interaction

- Heading/body: Inter; suggested 200–300ms interaction transitions.
- Validate visible focus, 4.5:1 light-mode contrast, reduced motion, and 375/768/1024/1440px layouts.
- Avoid emoji control icons and text-heavy layouts.

This is raw recommendation output, not an approved visual design. The landing-page pattern is not applicable to the in-room queue panel; the task design must instead preserve the existing room visual language and apply only its accessibility, responsive, state-feedback, and destructive-action guidance where appropriate.
