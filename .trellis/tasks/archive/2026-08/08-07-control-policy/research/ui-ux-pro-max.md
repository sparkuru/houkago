# UI/UX reference notes

Source: local `ui-ux-pro-max` search for room permission policy presets and
expandable advanced controls.

- Put the safe common choices first and reveal detailed controls progressively.
- Use a labelled native-style radio group for mutually exclusive presets, with
  a text status for selected and custom policies; do not communicate selection
  with colour alone.
- Keep every button at least 44px tall, with visible focus and keyboard order
  matching the layout. Do not rely on hover or drag interactions.
- Show policy-change feedback from the server-authoritative snapshot and retain
  clear nearby error/retry language if the request cannot be applied.
- On portrait phones stack options, preserve readable labels, and ensure the
  room panel does not introduce horizontal scrolling.
