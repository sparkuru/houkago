# UI/UX research for interaction-surface polish

## Repository and project evidence

- The parent task's approved direction is Warm Club 2.0: quiet, asset-light,
  cream/paper surfaces, warm ink, restrained wood accents, and semantic tokens.
- The previous room-shell child explicitly left dialog content and chat-composer
  polish for a later slice. Its validation established the room's responsive
  and cinema contracts, including the persistent desktop chat rail.
- Current owners are BushitsuView, EnmokuPlayer, ChatPanel, KengenPanel,
  BaiduConnectionDialog, and BaiduFileDialog. Existing browser specs cover the
  mobile sheet, cinema, governance dialogs, and provider flows.

## UUPM recommendation and decision

The local UI/UX Pro Max design-system query recommended Aurora gradients, a
blue/green palette, and hosted Noto fonts. Those choices conflict with the
approved Warm Club direction, existing cream/wood tokens, and the asset-light
policy; they are rejected as product direction.

Applicable general guidance: maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard
focus, semantic labels, distinct disabled/loading/error states, touch targets of
at least 44px, responsive layouts without horizontal overflow, purposeful
motion, and reduced-motion support. The existing project policy and tokens are
the implementation authority.
