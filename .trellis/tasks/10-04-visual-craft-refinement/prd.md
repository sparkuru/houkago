# Visual craft refinement

## Authorization and scope

The active user goal authorizes continuing implementation and iterative review
against Awwwards, Webby and FWA quality standards. This is a new presentation
initiative after the archived migration and real-environment acceptance; it does
not reopen either initiative. Preserve Warm Club identity and all server-owned
identity, room, media, permission and provider behavior.

The owner accepted visuals with `视觉通过`, then authorized submission and
normal task closure with `可以提交` on 2026-10-04. This explicit approval
supersedes the original no-automatic-commit/archive boundary for this task.

## Requirements

- Review the actual home and room experience across typography, whitespace,
  hierarchy, color, motion, microinteractions, responsiveness and originality.
- Give the entry a distinctive, coherent after-school architectural identity,
  with clear authentication and join/create priorities and configured site copy.
- Carry the same visual language into room headers, waiting state and controls
  without reducing player space, chat usability or existing room functionality.
- Preserve loading/error/anonymous/registration/authenticated entry states,
  pending locks, keyboard order, visible focus, 44px touch targets, readable
  contrast, safe insets and reduced motion.
- Verify desktop, phone, tablet, landscape and enlarged-text layouts in a real
  browser; review screenshots and iterate until no concrete visual defect remains.
- Validate existing entry/room/media behavior and repository quality checks.

## Boundaries

No deployment, push, credential use, new backend/protocol behavior, rebrand,
external tracking, new provider capability or automatic commit/archive.
Mobile is required; Chromium emulation does not claim Safari/device acceptance.
Award recognition itself cannot be established through local tests.

## Acceptance evidence

Task validation maps all eight visual dimensions to inspected renders and browser
behavior; automated checks prove flows and boundaries, while visual judgments
are identified as such. Completion remains unproven until the full surface review
and iterative quality audit finish.
